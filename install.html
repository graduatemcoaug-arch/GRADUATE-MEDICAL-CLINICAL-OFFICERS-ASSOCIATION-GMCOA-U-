Install GMCOA-U on Your Phone

Add GMCOA-U to your home screen for quick, app-like access — no app store needed. Follow the steps for your phone below.

📱 Android (Chrome)

✅ Installed! Find GMCOA-U on your home screen.

If the button above doesn't appear, install manually:

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right corner of Chrome)
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  3. Tap "Add" or "Install" to confirm

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

iPhone doesn't support one-tap install — follow these steps in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen)
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

Note: this only works in Safari, not Chrome, on iPhone.

Once installed, GMCOA-U appears as an app icon on your home screen — no browser address bar, faster loading, and works even with a weak connection.
